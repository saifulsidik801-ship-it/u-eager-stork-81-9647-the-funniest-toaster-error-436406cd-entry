One line, at most 120 characters: the error message a kitchen toaster
shows its owner when something goes wrong. Deadpan, tight, and fresh —
no "I'm toast" pun in sight.

Entry text:

> ERROR E-7: The bread refused to brown. It has standards. I do not.

`main.py` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`. An AI judge compares
entries two at a time on funniness, freshness and tightness.
